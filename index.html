About

Finance researcher working at the intersection of corporate finance and computational methods.

I am a PhD Researcher in Finance at Hanken School of Economics. My research interests include corporate financial risk, refinancing, capital structure, debt markets and textual analysis. I am particularly interested in combining large-scale financial datasets with information extracted from corporate disclosures to study questions that are difficult to observe using conventional accounting and market variables alone.
